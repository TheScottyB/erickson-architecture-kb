# 701 South Lee Street

The **lone surviving structure** of the [[Des Plaines Superblock]] urban-renewal scheme. A 10-story office tower designed by **[[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]]** in the late 1970s, distinguished by an exposed-aggregate precast concrete skin and a recessed glass curtain-wall base lifted on pilotis.

## Design Description

> "Exposed aggregate; precast panels; recessed spandrels; two-story recessed glass curtain wall base with pilotis; 10 stories."  
> — Illinois Historic Structures Inventory (Landmarks Illinois)

![[701-s-lee-st-b.jpg]]

## Status & Significance

The IHSI survey notes carry an unusually pointed comment:

> "This was a major urban renewal project; everything has been demolished except this bldg."

So 701 S. Lee is the **only surviving piece** of the [[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]]-designed [[Des Plaines Superblock]] — the [[Des Plaines Mall]] was demolished in 1998 and the surrounding parcels likewise cleared. That makes it the most accessible in-situ example of the firm's commercial vocabulary.
