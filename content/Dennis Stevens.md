# Dennis Stevens

Architect. Long-time partner of **[[Don Erickson]]** in **[[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]]**. Trained at Taliesin as Frank Lloyd Wright's "youngest apprentice." Continued designing after the firm wound down; later relocated to Boise, ID where he runs the **Robie Springs** development with his son and business partner Sean.

![[dennis-stevens-taliesin.jpg]]
*Dennis Stevens at Taliesin — source: [Modern Illinois](https://modernil.com/upcoming-cbb-tour-a-brief-conversation-with-dennis-stevens/)*

## Career

- Trained as the **youngest apprentice to Frank Lloyd Wright** at Taliesin
- Returned to the U.S. and joined [[Don Erickson]] in the **mid-1950s**
- Partnership ran until the **1990s**, when Erickson stepped back due to failing health
- Continued designing solo; later moved to **Boise, ID** to develop Robie Springs with son Sean

![[dennis-stevens-and-sean.jpg]]
*Dennis Stevens with son Sean outside the Robie Springs development, Boise, ID — source: [Modern Illinois](https://modernil.com/upcoming-cbb-tour-a-brief-conversation-with-dennis-stevens/)*

## Design Philosophy (in his own words)

> "We used mostly residential contractors, and we put together a team of people over time who we'd work with over and over, because they knew how we designed and how things needed to be built and how to deal with the parts of our designs that weren't traditional."

> "The Riverwoods house was one of the first we built with a Jamaican roof. The woman who I designed the house for rented space from a building of ours on Lee Street in Des Plaines. She got this piece of land in Riverwoods, and she asked if I'd work on a design for her, and I said 'Sure!'"

When asked if he would still design a Jamaican-roof home today:

> "Yes, sure, have them call me!"
