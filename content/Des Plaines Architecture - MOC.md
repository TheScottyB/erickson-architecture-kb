# Des Plaines Architecture — MOC

A map-of-content for the 1970s downtown Des Plaines, IL redevelopment work of architects **[[Don Erickson]]** and **[[Dennis Stevens]]**, working as **[[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]]**. Their firm was the design lead on the city's ambitious "Superblock" urban-renewal scheme, producing a cluster of late-modernist commercial buildings between 1974 and 1978.

> Cross-reference: see also [[Don-Erickson-Architecture]] for the *residential* Chalet Modern catalog (Winnetka & Park Ridge homes).

## People

- [[Don Erickson]] — partner; FLW-influenced organic modernist
- [[Dennis Stevens]] — partner; "youngest apprentice" to Frank Lloyd Wright at Taliesin

- [[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]] — formal partnership **1965** → mid-1992
