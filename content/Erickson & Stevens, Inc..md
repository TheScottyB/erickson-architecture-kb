# Erickson & Stevens, Inc.

Chicagoland architecture partnership operated by **[[Don Erickson]]** and **[[Dennis Stevens]]** from the **mid-1950s** until the **1990s**. Both partners were shaped by Frank Lloyd Wright's organic-modernist tradition (Stevens trained at Taliesin), and the firm's catalog blends MCM/Chalet Modern residential work with a notable cluster of late-modernist commercial buildings in **downtown [[Des Plaines Superblock|Des Plaines]]**.

## Practice Profile

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Founded (formal) | **1965** (collaborated earlier; formal incorporation 1965) |
| Wound down | mid-1992 (Don's fall from roof; illness diagnosis 1993) |
| Stevens post-firm | Continued solo; relocated to Boise, ID (Robie Springs development) |
| Office addresses | 640 Pearson St, Des Plaines · 1873 Busse Hwy, Des Plaines *(per original brief — not verified in research-pass sources; the Des Plaines Mall sat at 700 Pearson St, which may have led to confusion)* |
| Partnership style | Design+build practice using a stable bench of residential contractors |
| Geography | Chicagoland suburbs + Jamaica |

## Building Catalog

### Des Plaines (commercial / mixed-use)

- [[Lee Street Office Plaza (950 S Lee)]] — formerly *Le Ronde*, c. 1974
- [[701 South Lee Street]] — c. 1976–78, lone Superblock survivor
- [[Des Plaines Mall]] — 1976–77, demolished 1998
- The broader [[Des Plaines Superblock]] scheme

### Residential (selected)

Per Stevens' interview, the firm's residential work spanned **Riverwoods, Highland Park, Palatine, Winnetka, Wood Dale**, and **Jamaica**. The user's existing residential research is in [[Don-Erickson-Architecture]] (Winnetka 945 Old Green Bay Rd; Park Ridge 507 Park Plaine Ave).

### Commercial (other)

- A "Jamaican-roof" restaurant in **Mt. Prospect, IL** on River Road

## Design Vocabulary

- **Jamaican roof** (the firm's signature curve — "Some neighbors call it pagoda" because it resembles traditional Japanese rooflines)
- East–West gable skylights, diamond skylights
- Floor-to-ceiling stone fireplaces
- Post-and-beam construction
- Pecky cedar wood ceilings
- Wraparound decks, sunken living areas
- For Des Plaines commercial work: white brick / exposed aggregate concrete / precast panels / piloti elevations / coffered concrete ceilings (see individual building notes)
