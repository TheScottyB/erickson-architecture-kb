# Frank Lloyd Wright

American architect (1867–1959), founder of organic modernism and the Prairie School. Creator of [[Taliesin]] in Spring Green, Wisconsin and Taliesin West in Scottsdale, Arizona — both serving as his home, studio, and school for apprentices through the **Taliesin Fellowship**.

## Connection to Erickson & Stevens

Both [[Don Erickson]] and [[Dennis Stevens]] trained as Wright apprentices:
- **Don Erickson** — accepted Dec 15, 1948 at Taliesin West, studied 1948–1951. Personal friend; played piano for Mr. & Mrs. Wright in their Taliesin apartment.
- **Dennis Stevens** — Wright's **youngest-ever apprentice**, joining at age 17 in 1948.

The firm's design vocabulary — soaring volumes, natural light, post-and-beam, organic site integration — is a direct inheritance of Wright's principles, applied to their own invented forms (especially the [[Jamaican Roof]]).
