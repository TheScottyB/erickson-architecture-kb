# Indian Lakes Resort

**250 West Schick Road, Bloomingdale, IL 60108**

Don Erickson's most widely recognized public work — a resort hotel in Bloomingdale, Illinois whose design carried the signature FLW-derived organic modernist vocabulary of [[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]] into a major hospitality project.

> "The Wright stuff; Renovation of Indian Lakes Resort bears the stamp of Frank Lloyd Wright"
> — Katie Klabusich, *Press Publications/Northwest DuPage*, January 16, 2003

> "Indian Lakes has the 'Wright' touch; Four-star, Chicago-area gold resort completes $20 million renovation project"
> — *Chicago Sun-Times*, Special Advertising Supplement, 2005

## Press Coverage

- *Daily Herald* — "2000 Couples Later… Indian Lakes marks 25 years of weddings, receptions," December 18, 2005
- *Lodging Hospitality* — "Right place, right time: First Hospitality Group enlists The Gettys Group to restore sparkle to a local diamond in the rough," November 1, 2002
- *Chicago Sun-Times* — $20 million renovation supplement, 2005

## Significance

Indian Lakes is the work most frequently cited when Erickson's career is summarized — it gave his residential design vocabulary a public face at a hospitality scale. The *Chicago Tribune* obituary (2006) led with it: *"Architect designed Indian Lakes resort."*
