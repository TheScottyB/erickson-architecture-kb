# Lee Street Office Plaza (950 S Lee St)

Late-modernist office building in downtown **Des Plaines, IL**, designed by **[[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]]** ([[Don Erickson]] and [[Dennis Stevens]]). Originally branded **"Le Ronde"**, now operating as the **Lee Street Office Plaza**.

## Design Description

> "White brick in stack bond; lifted on piers w/parking beneath; coffered ceiling over parking; bronze glass windows; interior rotunda."  
> — Illinois Historic Structures Inventory (Landmarks Illinois)

The piloti elevation, exposed coffered concrete soffit, and bronze-tinted curtain glazing place this firmly in the late-modernist commercial idiom of the early 1970s. The interior **rotunda** is the likely origin of the original "Le Ronde" branding.

[[Dennis Stevens]] notes in his [Modern Illinois interview](https://modernil.com/upcoming-cbb-tour-a-brief-conversation-with-dennis-stevens/) that one of his Riverwoods residential clients **rented space from a building of ours on Lee Street in Des Plaines** — likely a reference to either this building or [[701 South Lee Street]].
