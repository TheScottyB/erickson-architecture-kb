# Taliesin

[[Frank Lloyd Wright]]'s combined home, studio, and school. Two campuses:
- **Taliesin** — Spring Green, Wisconsin (summer)
- **Taliesin West** — Scottsdale, Arizona (winter)

Apprentices lived and worked alongside Wright, learning by doing. The program — the **Taliesin Fellowship** — produced a generation of architects who carried Wright's organic modernist principles into independent practice.

## Erickson & Stevens Connection

Both partners of [[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]] trained here:

| Apprentice | Started | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[Don Erickson]] | Dec 15, 1948 | Taliesin West, Scottsdale. Accepted with cousin Richard Erickson as condition. Studied 1948–1951. |
| [[Dennis Stevens]] | 1948 | Wright's **youngest-ever apprentice** (age 17). Showed Wright an isometric drawing; Wright said "You did this?" and accepted him on the spot. |
