# The Round House

An early award-winning residential design by [[Don Erickson]], widely published in 1958 and recognized with the **AIA Homes for Better Living Award**.

> "This Architect Cuts Corners; Round House Wins Prize"
> — *Chicago American*, July 8, 1958

> "Wright Influenced this Luxury Home"
> — *Chicago Daily News*, July 18, 1958

## Awards

- **AIA 1958 Homes for Better Living Award** — Merit Award (sponsored by American Institute of Architects in cooperation with *House and Home* and *Life* magazines)
- **Des Plaines Chamber of Commerce Architectural Award** — Award of Merit, 1959

## Press

The Round House received significant press in 1958, the year Erickson's private practice was gaining momentum:
- *Chicago American* — July 8, 1958
- *Chicago Daily News* — July 18, 1958
- *Playboy* — featured in "A playboy pad; Exotica in Exurbia; A young Midwest exec creates his own tropical paradise in Illinois" (circa 1970s — possibly the same or a related home)

## Notes

The exact location of the Round House is not confirmed in available sources. Given Erickson's practice base in the northwest Chicago suburbs (Palatine, Mt. Prospect, Barrington area) circa 1958, it is likely located in that region. Further research needed.
