# Erickson & Stevens — Architecture Knowledge Base

An entity-graph knowledge base documenting the work of **[[Don Erickson]]** and **[[Dennis Stevens]]**, two [Frank Lloyd Wright](Frank%20Lloyd%20Wright) apprentices who shaped mid-century modern architecture across Chicagoland from the 1950s through the 1990s.

## People

- [[Don Erickson]] — architect, 1929–2006; Wright apprentice 1948–1951
- [[Dennis Stevens]] — architect; Wright's youngest-ever apprentice (Taliesin, 1948)
- [[Frank Lloyd Wright]] — mentor; organic-modernist master

## Firm

- [[Erickson & Stevens, Inc.]] — formal partnership 1965–1992; design+build practice

## Concepts / Signatures

- [[Jamaican Roof]] — the firm's defining invention; inverted gambrel with bubble skylight
- [[Taliesin]] — Frank Lloyd Wright's school where both partners trained
